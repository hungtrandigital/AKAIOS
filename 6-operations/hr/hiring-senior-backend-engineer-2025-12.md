## Role Definition

**Title & Level:** Senior Backend Engineer (IC4)

**Responsibilities:**
- Build and ship backend services with clear SLIs/SLOs (40%)
- Improve reliability, monitoring, and incident response (25%)
- Optimize performance (latency/throughput) across APIs and data paths (20%)
- Mentor peers, review code, document patterns (15%)

**Required Skills:**
- 5+ years backend experience (Python/Go/Node)
- Cloud (AWS/GCP/Azure) + containers (Docker/K8s)
- System design, scaling, reliability fundamentals
- SQL/NoSQL familiarity

**Nice-to-Have Skills:**
- Event-driven architectures; queues/streams
- Observability stacks (OpenTelemetry/Prometheus/Grafana)
- Security-minded (authN/Z, secrets)

**Reports To:** Head of Engineering
