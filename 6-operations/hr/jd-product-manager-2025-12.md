# Job Description — Product Manager (JD ID: OPS-TASK-005)

**Level/Band:** PM L3  
**Department/Team:** Product  
**Location/Type:** Remote-first (±3h PT preferred)  
**Reports To:** Head of Product  
**Employment Type:** Full-time  
**Parent Epic:** PRD-EPIC-PRODUCT-ROADMAP  
**Registry Link:** `../tracking/registry.md` (OPS-TASK-005)

## Mission (3-5 bullets)
- Drive roadmap delivery for [product area] with clear customer outcomes
- Translate customer insights into prioritized problems and crisp specs
- Align Engineering/Design/Go-to-market on goals, milestones, and success metrics

## Responsibilities (5-10 bullets)
- Own discovery for assigned product area: customer research, data analysis, problem validation
- Write PRDs/specs with clear acceptance criteria and success metrics
- Partner with Engineering on delivery sequencing, scope trade-offs, and launch quality
- Collaborate with Design for UX flows and validation
- Define and track product KPIs; run experiments to improve adoption/retention
- Communicate updates to stakeholders; maintain roadmap and status in source of truth

## Requirements — Must-Have
- 3–5 years PM experience shipping SaaS products
- Strong discovery skills: customer interviews, data-driven prioritization
- Excellent written specs; can break down problems into lean iterations
- Comfortable with analytics (funnels, cohorts); SQL or analytics tools familiarity
- Strong collaboration with Engineering/Design; clear communication

## Requirements — Nice-to-Have
- Domain experience: [industry/vertical]
- Experience with experimentation (A/B), feature flags
- Exposure to API products and developer experience
- Prior startup experience; thrives in fast-moving environment

## Success Metrics (see `../tracking/metrics-glossary.md`)
- Time to first shipped iteration: <6 weeks
- KPI lift in owned area: define target (e.g., activation, retention, conversion)
- Delivery predictability: % commitments delivered per cycle
- Stakeholder satisfaction: positive feedback from Eng/Design/GT

- Equity: [range], 4-year vest, 1-year cliff

## Interview Process & Timeline
- Phone screen → Product sense/case → Execution/analytics → Team/Panel → Leadership → References → Offer
- Assessments: Product case + execution deep dive; ~2-3 hours total
- Target Timeline: 4–6 weeks from posting to offer
- Decision Owners: Head of Product + CEO
