# Market Research Plans

This directory contains **research plans for deep research projects**.

## Purpose

Research plans are created **before executing deep research** to:
- Define research objectives and questions
- Specify research methods (qualitative, quantitative, competitive, trends)
- Plan timeline and phases
- Identify resource requirements
- Set success criteria

## When to Create Research Plans

Create a research plan when:
- ✅ Multiple research methods required (interviews + surveys + competitive)
- ✅ Large sample size (10+ interviews, 50+ survey responses, 5+ competitors)
- ✅ Timeline >2 days
- ✅ Requires coordination (recruiting, scheduling)
- ✅ Budget implications (tools, subscriptions, paid panels)

## Naming Convention

`[topic]-research-plan-[YYYY-MM].md`

**Examples:**
- `saas-analytics-market-research-plan-2025-12.md`
- `enterprise-segment-research-plan-2026-01.md`
- `competitor-deep-dive-research-plan-2025-12.md`

## Plan Template

See `shared/templates/` for research plan template (or refer to market-research agent workflow step 0.1).

## Workflow

1. **Plan** → Create plan in this directory
2. **Approve** → Get stakeholder approval
3. **Execute** → Follow plan and conduct research
4. **Document** → Create reports in `../reports/`
5. **Archive** → Move completed plans to `archives/` after 3 months

## Related Directories

- **Reports:** `../reports/` - Research findings and detailed reports
- **Summaries:** `../summaries.md` - Executive summaries of all research
- **Resources:** `../resources/` - Raw data, transcripts, reference materials
