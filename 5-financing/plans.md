## Finance Scale Rules
- Keep ≤ 10 active initiatives in this document; move older/paused items to Parking Lot below.
- Group initiatives by theme (Fundraising, Unit Economics, Cash Management, Profitability).
- Assign `FIN-TASK-XXX` IDs and link to `tracking/registry.md`.
- Update financial metrics in `tracking/registry.md` monthly.
- Groom monthly; resequence quarterly (budget replan); archive quarterly to `archives/YYYY-MM/` with actual vs. forecast comparison.
