# Finance Metrics Glossary

Standard definitions for financial metrics to ensure consistency across plans, projections, and reporting.

---

## Revenue & Growth

**MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)**  
Predictable revenue from subscriptions/contracts each month.  
Formula: Number of Customers × Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)  
Example: 100 customers × $500/month = $50,000 MRR

**ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue)**  
MRR annualized; commonly used to value SaaS companies.  
Formula: MRR × 12  
Example: $50,000 × 12 = $600,000 ARR

**ARPU (Average Revenue Per User)**  
Average revenue generated per customer per period.  
Formula: Total Revenue / Number of Customers  
Example: $50,000 MRR / 100 customers = $500 ARPU

**Net New MRR**  
New revenue added minus churned revenue in a period.  
Formula: (New Customers × ARPU) - (Churn Revenue)  
Example: (+$10,000 new) - ($2,000 churn) = $8,000 net new MRR

**Growth Rate (MoM / YoY)**  
Month-over-month or year-over-year percentage change in revenue.  
Formula: (Current Period - Previous Period) / Previous Period × 100  
Example: ($55,000 MRR - $50,000) / $50,000 = 10% MoM growth

## Customer Economics

**CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)**  
Total cost to acquire one paying customer.  
Formula: Total Marketing & Sales Spend / Number of New Customers Acquired  
Example: $50,000 spend / 100 customers = $500 CAC

**LTV (Customer Lifetime Value)**  
Total revenue expected from one customer over their entire relationship.  
Formula: (ARPU × Customer Lifespan in Months) - (Cost to Serve)  
Example: ($500 ARPU × 24 months) - $1,000 overhead = $11,000 LTV

**CAC Payback Period**  
Months required to recover the CAC from a customer's revenue.  
Formula: CAC / Monthly Contribution Margin (ARPU - COGS)  
Example: $500 CAC / $400 margin = 1.25 months

**LTV:CAC Ratio**  
Health metric comparing lifetime value to acquisition cost.  
Target: ≥ 3:1 (each $1 spent acquires $3 in lifetime value)  
Example: $11,000 LTV / $500 CAC = 22:1 (very healthy)

**Churn Rate (Monthly)**  
Percentage of customers lost each month.  
Formula: (Customers Lost This Month / Starting Customers) × 100  
Example: 10 customers lost / 100 starting = 10% monthly churn

**Net Dollar Retention (NDR)**  
Revenue retained + expansion from existing customers (excluding new logos).  
Formula: (Beginning MRR + Expansion - Churn) / Beginning MRR  
Target: > 100% means customers spend more over time  
Example: ($50,000 + $5,000 expansion - $2,000 churn) / $50,000 = 106% NDR

## Burn & Runway

**Burn Rate (Monthly)**  
Rate at which the company spends cash each month.  
Formula: (Cash Spent This Month - Revenue This Month) = Net Burn  
Example: $100,000 expenses - $50,000 revenue = $50,000 monthly burn

**Gross Burn**  
Total operating expenses per month (regardless of revenue).  
Example: $100,000/month in payroll, infra, marketing = $100,000 gross burn

**Net Burn**  
Gross burn minus revenue in the same period.  
Example: $100,000 gross - $50,000 revenue = $50,000 net burn

**Runway**  
Months of operations the company can sustain with current burn and cash balance.  
Formula: Current Cash Balance / Monthly Net Burn  
Example: $1,000,000 cash / $50,000 burn = 20 months runway

**Payback Month**  
Month at which cumulative cash flow becomes positive (reaches break-even).  
Example: Month 18 (if funded in Month 1, break-even in Month 18)

---

## Unit Economics

**COGS (Cost of Goods Sold)**  
Direct costs to deliver the product/service per customer.  
Example: Cloud infrastructure, payment processing, support = $50/customer/month

**Gross Profit / Gross Margin %**  
Revenue minus COGS.  
Formula: (Revenue - COGS) / Revenue × 100  
Example: ($500 ARPU - $50 COGS) / $500 = 90% gross margin

**Contribution Margin**  
Revenue available to cover fixed costs and profit after variable costs.  
Formula: ARPU - COGS - Variable Marketing Spend  
Example: $500 - $50 - $100 variable CAC = $350 contribution margin

**CAC Efficiency Ratio**  
Marketing spend as % of revenue generated (lower is better).  
Formula: (Total CAC / Total Revenue in Period) × 100  
Example: $500 CAC / $500 ARPU = 100% (very high; improve efficiency)

## Fundraising & Valuation

**Valuation (Pre-Money / Post-Money)**  
Pre-Money: Company value before funding round  
Post-Money: Company value including new funding  
Formula: Post-Money = Pre-Money + Funding Amount  
Example: $5M pre × $2M raise = $7M post-money valuation

**Dilution**  
Percentage of company ownership given away in a funding round.  
Formula: (Funding Amount / Post-Money Valuation) × 100  
Example: $2M raise / $7M post = 28.6% dilution

**Months to Profitability**  
Projected month at which the company reaches cash-flow break-even without additional funding.  
Example: 18 months at current growth rate

---

## Benchmarks & Health Checks

**Healthy SaaS Metrics (varies by stage):**
- CAC Payback: < 12 months
- LTV:CAC: > 3:1
- NDR: > 100%
- Gross Margin: > 60%
- Runway: > 12 months (investor confidence)

**Growth Red Flags:**
- Churn > 5% monthly (unsustainable)
- CAC growing while LTV flat (efficiency problem)
- Runway < 6 months (urgent fundraising needed)
- Burn accelerating without proportional revenue growth

## Acronyms Quick Reference

- ARR = Annual Recurring Revenue
- ARPU = Average Revenue Per User
- CAC = Customer Acquisition Cost
- COGS = Cost of Goods Sold
- LTV = Lifetime Value
- MRR = Monthly Recurring Revenue
- NDR = Net Dollar Retention
- MoM = Month-over-Month
- YoY = Year-over-Year
