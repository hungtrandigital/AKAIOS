## Workflow

1. **Review Initial Plan** - Start with [1-ideas/finance/initial-financing-plan.md](../1-ideas/finance/initial-financing-plan.md) (initial version)
2. **Develop Detailed Plans** - Create comprehensive plans in [plans.md](plans.md)
3. **Create Projections** - Build financial models in [projections/](projections/)
4. **Prepare Pitches** - Develop pitch materials in [pitches/](pitches/)

## Lifecycle & Structure
- Keep `plans.md` as the single source of truth for strategy and active initiatives; link out to projections and pitch materials.
- Cap active initiatives to ≤ 10 in plans; move paused/old items to a Parking Lot section.
- Track work items via IDs in `tracking/registry.md` (FIN-TASK-XXX) and mirror financial metrics monthly.
- Groom monthly (update metrics), resequence quarterly (budget replan), archive quarterly to `archives/YYYY-MM/` with actual vs. forecast comparison.
- **Versioning:** Log major strategy updates in `history/changelog.md`; snapshot plans quarterly to `archives/YYYY-MM/plans-snapshot.md`. Keep plans as working copy.

## Tracking & Reports
- `tracking/registry.md` — Financial initiatives and key metrics
- `tracking/metrics-glossary.md` — Standard metric definitions (MRR, ARR, CAC, LTV, runway, burn)
- Link significant financial outcomes/results from plans to registry for continuity.
