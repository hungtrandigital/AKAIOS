## Workflow

1. **Define Personas** - Start with [personas.md](personas.md) to understand your audience
2. **Develop Strategy** - Create comprehensive GTM strategy in [go-to-market.md](go-to-market.md)
3. **Plan Campaigns** - Add campaign sections in `go-to-market.md` and use [templates/campaign-brief-template.md](templates/campaign-brief-template.md) when a separate brief is justified
4. **Track Performance** - Record metrics and campaign status in [performance/](performance/)
5. **Link Assets** - Store or reference campaign assets from [shared/assets/](../shared/assets/README.md) instead of inventing parallel creative folders

## Lifecycle & Structure
- Keep `go-to-market.md` as the single source of truth for strategy and active campaigns; link out only when a separate brief or asset is genuinely needed.
- Cap active campaigns to ≤ 10 in GTM; move paused/past campaigns to a Parking Lot section.
- Group initiatives by themes (Launch, SEO, Paid, Partnerships) and maintain a short TOC when counts grow.
- Track work items via IDs in `3-technical/3.2-implementation/status/work-items-registry.md` (MKT-TASK-XXX) and mirror key campaign status in `performance/`.
- Keep assets in `shared/assets/` and use `templates/` only for reusable planning scaffolds, not as a dumping ground for live campaign state.
- Groom weekly (prune stale), resequence monthly (budget/ROAS), archive quarterly to `archives/YYYY-MM/` with outcomes.
- **Versioning:** Log major strategy updates in `history/gtm-changelog.md`; snapshot GTM quarterly to `archives/YYYY-MM/gtm-snapshot.md` for historical reference. Keep GTM as working copy.

## Tracking & Reports
