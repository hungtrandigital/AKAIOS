# Marketing Metrics Glossary

Standard definitions to ensure consistency across campaigns and reporting.

---

## Customer Acquisition

**CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)**  
Average cost to acquire one paying customer.  
Formula: Total Marketing Spend / Number of New Customers  
Example: $10,000 spend / 50 customers = $200 CAC

**CPA (Cost Per Action / Cost Per Acquisition)**  
Cost to achieve one desired action (lead, signup, download, etc.).  
Formula: Total Ad Spend / Number of Actions  
Example: $5,000 spend / 100 leads = $50 CPA

**CPL (Cost Per Lead)**  
Same as CPA when the action is a lead.  
Formula: Total Ad Spend / Number of Leads

---

## Revenue & Profitability

**LTV (Lifetime Value)**  
Total revenue expected from a customer over their relationship.  
Formula: (Average Purchase Value × Purchase Frequency × Customer Lifespan)  
Example: $50/month × 12 months × 2 years = $1,200 LTV

**ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)**  
Revenue generated per dollar spent on ads.  
Formula: Revenue from Ads / Ad Spend  
Example: $50,000 revenue / $10,000 spend = 5x ROAS (or 500%)

**CAC Payback Period**  
Months to recover CAC from customer revenue.  
Formula: CAC / (Monthly Revenue per Customer)  
Example: $200 CAC / $50/month revenue = 4 months

---

## Traffic & Engagement

**Impressions**  
Number of times ad is displayed (not clicked).  
Platform-specific: varies by channel.

**Clicks / Click-through Rate (CTR)**  
CTR = Clicks / Impressions  
Example: 500 clicks / 50,000 impressions = 1% CTR

**Conversion Rate**  
% of visitors/clickers who complete desired action.  
Formula: Conversions / Total Visitors or Clicks  
Example: 10 leads / 100 clicks = 10% conversion rate

**Bounce Rate**  
% of visitors who leave without taking action.  
Formula: (Sessions with One Page / Total Sessions) × 100

---

## Audience & Reach

**Reach**  
Unique number of people exposed to ad (vs. impressions, which count repeats).

**Frequency**  
Average number of times each person sees the ad.  
Formula: Impressions / Reach

**Audience Size**  
Total number of people in targeting segment.  
Platform-specific; varies by filters.

---

## Attribution & Reporting

**View-Through Conversion (VTC)**  
Conversion from someone who saw (but didn't click) the ad.  
Harder to track; depends on platform.

**Click-Through Conversion (CTC)**  
Conversion from someone who clicked the ad.  
Easiest to track.

**Multi-touch Attribution**  
Credit distributed across multiple touchpoints (ad → click → landing page → signup).  
Example: First touch (40%), Last touch (60%).

---

## Benchmarks & Health Checks

**Healthy ROAS Range** (varies by industry)  
- SaaS: 4x–10x ROAS
- E-commerce: 2x–5x ROAS
- B2B Lead Gen: 3x–8x ROAS

**CAC Payback Sweet Spot**  
- Ideal: < 12 months  
- Acceptable: 12–18 months  
- At Risk: > 18 months

---

## Channel-Specific Terms

**Meta (Facebook/Instagram)**  
- Relevance Score (1–10): Quality of ad for audience
- Quality Ranking: How well ad performs vs. competitors
- Engagement Rate: (Likes + Comments + Shares) / Impressions

**Google Ads**  
- Quality Score (1–10): Keyword relevance + landing page quality
- Expected CTR: Historical + estimated click-through
- Search Impression Share: % of available searches your ad showed for

**LinkedIn**  
- Engagement Rate: (Reactions + Comments + Shares) / Impressions
- Account Opening Rate: % who visit company page
- Lead Form Submission Rate: % who fill lead form
